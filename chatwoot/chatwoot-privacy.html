Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how our team support application ("we", "our", or "us") handles data when users communicate with our official Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts through our integrated customer service platform (Chatwoot).

2. Information We Collect

When you send a direct message, reply to a story, or interact with our connected social channels, we may receive:

  • Your public profile identifier (such as your username, scoped ID, or page-scoped ID).
  • The text, links, and media attachments sent within the direct message conversation.
  • Timestamps associated with your messages.

3. How We Use Information

Data received through Meta APIs is used strictly to:

  • Receive, read, and respond to direct inquiries submitted by participants and users.
  • Provide technical and general event support during operations.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your message data with third-party advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain conversation records only as long as necessary to provide support and resolve inquiries. Users may request full deletion of their conversation history at any time by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out directly via direct message on our official social media channels.
