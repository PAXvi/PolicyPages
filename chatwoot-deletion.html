User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform policies, users have the right to request deletion of their data collected through our Facebook and Instagram messaging integrations.

How to Request Data Deletion:
  1. Send a Direct Message from the account you wish to have purged with the text: DELETE MY DATA.
  2. Alternatively, remove our application's access via your Meta account:
    • Go to your Facebook or Instagram Settings.
    • Navigate to Apps and Websites.
    • Find our application and click Remove.

What Happens Next

Upon receiving a deletion request, our administrators will permanently delete your contact profile, conversation history, and associated identifiers from our Chatwoot platform database within 48 hours.
